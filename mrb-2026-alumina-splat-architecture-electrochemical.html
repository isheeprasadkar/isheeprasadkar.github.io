Role of splat architecture for enhanced electrochemical performance in the bond-coat-free plasma-sprayed alumina coatings

K. Srivastava, S. Kumar, I.P. Kar, N.K. Singh, A.K. Keshri
Materials Research Bulletin, 114347 · 2026
Read the paper (DOI)CV

In brief

Shows how the shape and stacking of the solidified droplets (splats) in alumina coatings, sprayed without a bond coat, control their electrochemical behaviour.
