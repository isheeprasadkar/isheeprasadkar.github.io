Phase-engineered WC-based freestanding electrode for hydrogen evolution: A substitute to nanostructured electrocatalysts

I.P. Kar, S. Kumar, N. Pandit, C.K. Nayak, A.K. Keshri
International Journal of Hydrogen Energy, 264, 156894 · 2026
Read the paper (DOI)CV

In brief

Uses plasma spraying to build a self-supporting tungsten-carbide electrode whose crystal phases are tuned for producing hydrogen from water, offering a simpler, scalable alternative to complex nanostructured catalysts.
