Dielectric reliability and mechanical integrity of bond-coat-free alumina coatings by atmospheric plasma spraying

S. Kumar, I.P. Kar, N.K. Singh, A.K. Keshri
Ceramics International · 2025
Read the paper (DOI)CV

In brief

Tests how reliably plasma-sprayed alumina coatings insulate electrically, and how mechanically sound they are, when applied without a bond coat.
