Enhancement in the Hot Corrosion Resistance of Plasma-Sprayed NiAl Coating Through Nanodiamond Reinforcement

In brief

Reinforces plasma-sprayed NiAl coatings with nanodiamonds to make them resist hot corrosion at high temperature.
