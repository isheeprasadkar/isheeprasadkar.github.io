Graphene-reinforced WC-CoCr coatings by high-velocity air fuel spraying: a path to superior corrosion protection

I.P. Kar, S. Kumar, N.K. Singh, S. Sharma, S.M. Pandey, A.K. Keshri
Journal of Thermal Spray Technology, 34 (6), 2094–2107 · 2025
Read the paper (DOI)CV

In brief

Adds graphene to hard WC-CoCr cermet coatings sprayed by HVAF and shows the resulting coatings protect steel against corrosion far better.
