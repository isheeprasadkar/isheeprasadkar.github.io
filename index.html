About

Physicist turned materials engineer.

I am a doctoral researcher in the Department of Metallurgical and Materials Engineering at IIT Patna, working in the Plasma Spray Coating Laboratory under the supervision of Prof. Anup Kumar Keshri. My background is in physics (M.Sc., NIT Durgapur), which I now apply to processing–structure–property questions in sprayed materials.

My research asks how high-temperature plasma processing can be used not only to deposit coatings, but to engineer materials — controlling phases and microstructure — so that they perform as efficient, self-supported electrodes for hydrogen production.

Research interests

Four connected threads, all built around spray processing.

Plasma & thermal spray processing

Atmospheric plasma spraying as a scalable route to coatings and freestanding electrodes, with control over phase and microstructure.

Electrocatalysis for hydrogen evolution

Low-cost, binder-free HER electrodes based on transition-metal carbides as alternatives to noble-metal catalysts.

Functional ceramic coatings

Bond-coat-free plasma-sprayed alumina coatings for dielectric insulation and electrochemical performance.

Protective & corrosion-resistant coatings

Graphene-nanoplatelet-reinforced WC–CoCr and nanodiamond-reinforced NiAl coatings for corrosion, hot-corrosion and molten-salt protection.
